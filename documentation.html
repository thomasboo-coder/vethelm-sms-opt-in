Text opt-in documentation

Veterinary clinic staff opt in by texting the keyword BOOK to (866) 401-9840. VetHelm replies only in that conversation to book a mobile ultrasound. VetHelm does not send marketing texts and does not text pet owners.

Keyword campaign

  • Number: (866) 401-9840

  • Frequency: varies based on the booking
  • HELP returns help. STOP cancels further texts.
